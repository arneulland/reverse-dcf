How it works

The first row is today: your starting amount plus any extra you enter for this year. Each following year, the balance grows by the annual return, and then the yearly addition and any extra are added at year end. A real portfolio won't earn the same return every year, and the order of good and bad years matters once you add or withdraw money. Treat the result as a direction, not a forecast.
