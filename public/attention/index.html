    How it works

    Each source is scored on a log scale, because attention is lopsided: a few stocks get almost all of it. Wikipedia earns 0 points at 10 daily views and 100 at 20,000. News earns 100 at 100 stories a week, which is also the most the Google News feed returns. Reddit earns 100 at 1,000 mentions a day across stock forums, and StockTwits at a million watchers. Reddit and StockTwits weigh most, since the question is how much people talk about the stock. They only cover US listings, so stocks listed elsewhere are scored on Wikipedia and news alone.

    Trend compares the last 30 days of Wikipedia views with the past year, this week's news with the last 90 days, and today's Reddit mentions with yesterday's. Rising means at least 25% above normal, and Falling at least 20% below.

    From Torghatten Capital. Sources: Wikimedia page views, Google News, ApeWisdom (Reddit) and StockTwits. Results are cached for up to six hours.
